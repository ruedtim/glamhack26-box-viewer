What SIK-ISEA adds to SKKG's records

SIK-ISEA's research portal holds 347 work records owned by SKKG, many from its catalogues raisonnés. 298 of them were linked to an SKKG object (237 by the SKKG inventory number, 61 by artist and title). For each, SIK-ISEA's exhibitions (one per venue) and its ownership stations at institutions were compared with SKKG's knowledge graph (KG), and the labels on the back were searched for the same exhibitions and houses. This page shows only the numbers, the links to the SIK-ISEA records and our own label evidence; the details are in SIK-ISEA's records (their content is not republished here).

Evidence: strong = issuer/town and year (or lot/catalogue number) on the label; issuer only = the house's name without a date; same touring show, other venue; place only = town without a year, a weak lead. Readings come from SKKG's label register, its provenance data sheets and OCR of the backs. Private owners are never named. Catalogue pages at Heidelberg University Library are linked, not reproduced.
