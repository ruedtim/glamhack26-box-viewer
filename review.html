How to review

A vision-language model proposed a box for the marks described in SKKG's provenance data sheets that the text recognition could not find. The white dashed frame on the whole back is the area the data sheet names.

If the mark is visible but the box is wrong or too loose, use Redraw box and drag the right one; it is then shown in green. After a decision the next open proposal follows.

Keys: Y correct · N wrong · U unsure · R redraw · ← → move.

Names appear as the records and labels give them; addresses are masked («[address]»).

Your decisions stay in this browser: download them when you are done (or before you leave) and put the file into the team's folder; they are merged with everyone else's. More in About →
