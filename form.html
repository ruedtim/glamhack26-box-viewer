Record a label

Form for columns F to L of SKKG's label register. What the data already know is prefilled and marked: add to it or correct it. The panel on the right shows the row as it will go into the register. The register is German, so the terms you pick (shape, colour, support, event, evidence) and the column headings stay German; the English glosses are for orientation.
