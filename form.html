Record a label

Columns F to L of SKKG's label register, prefilled from the data: check, correct, add.

How this form works
  • What the data already know is prefilled and marked: add to it or correct it. Every change is saved in this browser.
  • The preview (beside the fields; on a phone the bar at the bottom) shows the row as it will go into the register. «Copy row» copies it for pasting into column F of the Excel register.
  • The register is German, so the terms you pick (shape, colour, support, event, evidence) and the column headings stay German; the English glosses are for orientation.
  • The work named at the top (columns A to E) is not edited here.
  • Names appear as the records and labels give them; addresses are masked («[address]»).

Prefilled fields are marked: Register or Data sheet = taken from the data, Suggestion = derived, or found by the image search: to check, edited ↺ = changed by hand (↺ restores the value from the data). Where the values come from is shown below each section.
